Run the Answer Engine Optimization track on its own. Use this when the question
is specifically "why don't AI assistants mention us?" rather than a full search
engagement.

1. Read `input/site-profile.md`. Use `$1` as the domain if supplied.

2. Launch `seo-recon` and wait — the AEO agents need the page inventory.

3. Launch in parallel:
   - `crawler-access-engineer` — can AI bots reach the site at all?
   - `rendering-specialist` — can they see anything once they arrive?
   - `entity-grounding-specialist` — is the brand a resolvable entity?
   - `conversational-query-researcher` — what are people actually asking?

   These four are ordered by how fatal they are. A site that fails the first two
   cannot be fixed by better content, and that is the most common finding.

4. Then `aeo-strategist` (synthesizes the above), then in parallel
   `ai-brand-monitor` and `answer-extractability-engineer`.

5. Report in chat:
   - **The AEO scorecard** — 1-5 on crawler access, render visibility,
     extractability, entity strength, off-site presence, original assets
   - **The blocker**, if there is one — the single thing preventing citation.
     Usually it is rendering or crawler access, and everything else is wasted
     effort until it is fixed.
   - **What models currently say about the brand**, verbatim, with any factual
     errors flagged as urgent
   - **Citation share** against competitors
   - **The fix sequence**, in dependency order

Be direct about the ceiling. If the site is client-side rendered and blocked at
the CDN, no amount of content work will produce citations — say that in the first
paragraph rather than burying it under recommendations.
