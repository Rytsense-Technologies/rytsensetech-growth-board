description: Monthly SEO cycle — refresh data, monitor AI citations, catch decay, report on progress against the plan.
---

The recurring operating cadence, not a one-off audit. Run this monthly once
`/seo-360` has established the baseline and plan.

1. Read `output/EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md` and `output/12-project-plan.md` to recall
   what was committed to. If neither exists, say so and suggest `/seo-360` first.

2. **Refresh the data** — parallel: `gsc-data-analyst`, `ga4-data-analyst`,
   `index-coverage-analyst`.

3. **Monitor** — parallel: `ai-brand-monitor` (same prompt set, unmodified —
   a changed prompt set cannot show a trend), `serp-landscape-analyst` on the
   priority queries, `content-refresh-specialist` for new decay.

4. **Report** via `seo-analyst`, writing `output/reports/<YYYY-MM>-report.md`:
   - Movement against each KPI, versus last month and versus baseline
   - What shipped from the plan, and what it produced
   - What did not ship, and why
   - New decay, new opportunities, new competitor moves
   - AI citation share trend
   - Anything that needs a decision

5. **Adjust** — if results diverge materially from `output/18-forecast.md`,
   invoke `seo-director` to reassess. A plan that is not working should change,
   and month three is when that usually becomes visible.

Keep it short. A monthly report nobody reads is worse than no report. Lead with
what changed and what needs a decision; put the full tables below.
